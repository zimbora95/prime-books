understanding, communication, fitness, and personal reflection.

## Collective Sports Assessment

**At the end of each half-term or full term, students will complete a practical performance**
**assessment in the team sport studied during that period (e.g. Futsal, Basketball or**
Volleyball).

Students will be assessed on their ability to:

•
Demonstrate correct technical skills.
•
Apply tactical understanding during game situations.
•
Make appropriate decisions under pressure.
•
Communicate effectively with teammates.
•
Show teamwork, cooperation, and leadership.
•
Respect the rules, officials, teammates, and opponents.
•
Demonstrate fair play and positive sporting behaviour.

Assessment is based on performance during authentic game situations rather than isolated
drills, encouraging students to apply their learning in realistic sporting environments.


## Physical Fitness Assessment

Students will also complete a series of physical fitness assessments designed to monitor their
overall physical development.

The fitness assessment programme includes:

**Fitness Test**
**Component Assessed**
**Push-Up Test**
Upper body muscular strength and endurance
**Sit-Up Test**
Core muscular strength and endurance
**Beep Test (20 m Shuttle Run) Cardiovascular endurance and aerobic fitness**

These assessments provide measurable evidence of each student's physical fitness and
contribute towards their overall Physical Education achievement.

Students are encouraged to improve their personal performance throughout the year rather
than simply comparing themselves with others.

## Attendance and Alternative Assessment

Regular attendance and active participation are essential components of Physical Education.

Students who miss a significant number of practical lessons may not have sufficient evidence
for practical assessment.

**In these circumstances, students will complete a written assessment based on the sports**
studied during the relevant term.

The written assessment may include:

•
Rules and regulations
•
Technical skills
•
Tactical understanding
•
Safety procedures
•
Equipment and playing areas
•
Sports terminology
•
Application of strategies in game situations

**The written assessment will be marked out of 100%, and the result will contribute towards**
the student's overall achievement where practical evidence is unavailable.

## In This Unit You Will

**Learning Objectives for Unit 12**

By the end of this unit, you will be able to:

✔ Understand the history, development, rules, and core principles of handball.

✔ Perform advanced handball techniques including passing, catching, dribbling, shooting,

continuously, creating attacking opportunities and defending effectively within a constantly
changing game environment.

Throughout this unit, you will progressively develop advanced technical skills through
structured drills, tactical activities, conditioned games, and competitive match play while
improving physical fitness, communication, confidence, leadership, and resilience.

## UNIT 1

## FUTSAL

## In This Unit You Will

**Learning Objectives for Unit 1**

By the end of this unit, you will be able to:

✔ Demonstrate an advanced understanding of the rules, tactics, and principles of futsal.

✔ Identify court markings, player roles, formations, substitutions, and competition
regulations.

✔ Perform advanced techniques including first touch, dribbling, passing, shooting,
goalkeeping, and defensive play.

✔ Apply attacking and defensive tactical systems effectively during competitive match
situations.

✔ Demonstrate leadership, communication, teamwork, decision-making, and fair play.

✔ Participate confidently and safely in modified and competitive futsal matches.

✔ Evaluate personal and team performance using reflection and performance analysis.


## Main Idea

Futsal is a high-intensity indoor invasion game that demands exceptional technical ability,
rapid decision-making, creativity, and tactical intelligence. Played on a smaller court with
fewer players and a reduced-bounce ball, futsal encourages quick transitions, close ball
control, intelligent movement, and continuous teamwork.

Throughout this unit, you will refine advanced technical skills through structured drills,
tactical scenarios, conditioned games, and competitive match play while improving agility,
coordination, communication, leadership, and overall sporting performance.
