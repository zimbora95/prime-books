## Über dieses Buch

About this book

Welcome to your German journey! This book takes you from your very first

Hallo! to confident A2 German, step by step, through twelve units.

You follow Anna, Tom and their friends from the first greeting to

festivals, holidays and everything in between.

**WHAT MAKES THIS BOOK SPECIAL**

Twelve units from CEFR A1 to A2, made for young learners

Listening, speaking, reading and writing in every unit

QR-code audio places for every listening activity

Support, Core and Challenge tasks in every Wortwerkstatt

Reviews, a picture dictionary and a German portfolio at the end

**FOR THE CLASSROOM**

Use this book with the whole class, in pairs or alone.

Every unit ends with ICH KANN so you can see your progress.

Tick your own checkboxes - you are the captain of this journey!

## Hören - Listening

Anna meets her new friend Tom.

**[QR CODE - AUDIO]**

**1. Listen and point. Who speaks first - Anna or Tom?**

**Anna:**
Hallo! Ich heiße Anna. Wie heißt du?

**Tom:**
Hallo, Anna! Ich heiße Tom.

**Anna:**
Wie geht es dir?

**Tom:**
Gut, danke! Und dir?

**Anna:**
Ich bin froh! Tschüss, Tom!

**Tom:**
Bis bald, Anna!

**2. Listen again and circle the correct word.**

How is Anna at the end?    froh        traurig

How is Tom?    gut          müde

Play the audio twice: first listen only, then listen and circle.

**TEACHER / AUDIO SCRIPT**

Read the dialogue aloud slowly, twice. Swap voices for Anna and Tom.

Pause after each line so the class can echo it in chorus.

Answer key (activity 2): Anna - froh. Tom - gut.

Answer key (activity 1): Anna speaks first.
