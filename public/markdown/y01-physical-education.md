**PHYSICAL EDUCATION · YEAR 1**
## About this book

This book is for children in Year 1, about five to six years old, and for the
teachers and families who move with them.

Physical Education in Year 1 is not a sport exam. It is how a body learns to share
a space, to stop when asked, to try a jump again, and to be kind while it does
those things.

Children will practise walking, running, jumping, hopping, skipping, rolling,
throwing, catching, kicking and balancing. They will dance, play simple team
games, and notice what their body does when it works hard.

**SUCCESS IS TRYING**

The activities are written so a child can join in with confidence. Success is
trying, looking after a partner, and leaving the space tidy.

Prime Books Physical Education Year 1 is a complete student book for the first year of primary
school. It covers moving well, understanding movement, moving creatively, taking part, taking
responsibility and healthy bodies, matching the school's Year 1 Physical Education scheme. Short
teaching, unique watercolour plates, safety panels and play tasks sit on every topic.

**UNIT 1 · TOPIC 1.1 · WALK, RUN AND STOP**
## Walk, run and stop

A space is a gap you can see. In Year 1, a good space is one you could lie down in
without touching anyone.

Walk uses both feet, one after the other. Run uses both feet too, but there is a
moment when both are off the floor. Stop means freeze: feet still, eyes on the
teacher.

**MOVE IT**

Walk anywhere for one minute. Change direction when you meet somebody. Do
not touch. Then stand still.

**WATCH ME TRY**

Pip runs. Bramble calls stop. Pip's feet finish the step they started,
then they stay. He does not skid into Sorrel.

**SAFETY FIRST**

Look where you are going. Leave a lie-down space. Stop means stop, even if the
game is exciting.

**TRY IT**

**1**
Walk the space. Count how many children you can see without turning your
head.

**2**
Run to a cone and stop before you touch it. Do this four times.

**3**
When the teacher says stop, freeze. Tick if your feet were still.

**LOOK WHAT I CAN DO**

I left a space.

I stopped when I was asked.

**UNIT 1 · TOPIC 1.2 · THE JOIN**
## The join

A hop is a spring on one foot, landing on the same foot. A skip is a step and a hop,
repeating. To join them, you finish the hop and start the skip with no long pause
in the middle.

**WATCH ME TRY**

Tuft hops three times on his right foot. Then he skips to the cone. The
join is the third landing, which becomes the first skip.

**SAFETY FIRST**

Land softly, with a little bend in the knee. If you wobble, put the other foot
down. That is still good work.

**TRY IT**

**1**
Hop four times on each foot. Hold a wall if you need to.

**2**
Skip to a cone and skip back.

**3**
Join them: hop, hop, skip, skip. Draw the order.

**MY SEQUENCE**

**CHALLENGE**

Do hop-hop-skip across the meadow without stopping in the middle. A partner
watches the join.

**Picture 1.3 Sorrel sprints. Tuft creeps. Same meadow, two speeds. Look at how high each body is.**

**MOVE IT**
