## About This Book

This is your German book for Year 2. It is a B1 First Language book:

you already speak German every day, and now the book helps you to

read and write it too.

Inside you will find 19 units. Each unit has new words, a little story

to read, writing practice and a fun activity. There are reviews, a

picture-word list and your own portfolio at the end.

The B1 label means the language around you stays rich and lovely:

beautiful words, real questions, proper reasons (weil, darum, deshalb).

The reading and writing always stay small and friendly, just right

for hands that are still learning to form letters.

**YEAR 2 — B1 — FIRST LANGUAGE**

B1 = language richness. Year 2 = how we read and write.

## What does B1 mean here?

**A1 / A2**
First tiny words and sentences for new learners.

**B1 — this book**
You can understand and say a lot. Now the words get richer.

**B2**
Later: long texts and discussions, when you are older.

This book is B1 FIRST LANGUAGE: German is your home language.

You understand rich spoken words long before you can write them,

and that is exactly right. So the book gives you a big, beautiful

vocabulary through stories, songs and talk, while the reading and

writing steps stay small and joyful.
