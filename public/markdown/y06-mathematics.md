◎INTRODUCTION

Welcome to Year 6 mathematics

This Student Book is your guide for mathematics in Year 6 at Prime School. It

follows the Prime School Year 6 mathematics scheme of work for the full year:

numbers, shapes, calculation, data, fractions, probability, ratio and transformation.

**Each unit opens with what you will learn. Inside a unit, each topic teaches one idea**

carefully: a short explanation, at least one worked example, a second way to check,

then practice you write in your exercise book.

You will meet decimals to three places, negative numbers, area, averages and more.

The ideas build on Year 5. When something is new, the book shows the steps slowly

before asking you to try.

protractor, a pair of compasses or a calculator when your teacher says so.

Work carefully. Check your answers. Talk through hard questions with a partner

before you write. Mathematics in this book is something you do, not only something

you read.
