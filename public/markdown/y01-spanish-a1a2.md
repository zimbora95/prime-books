## ¡Hola! Vamos a empezar.

A first Spanish course for young beginners, following
the CEFR A1 and A2 steps.
**H O W I T W O R K S**

**Listen**

Hear it first, then say it.

**Speak**

Say it to a partner.

**Read**

A short story in every unit.

**Write**

A line, then a sentence.

**Your turn**

The page is yours: write and
draw on it.

**I can…**

Colour in what you can do.

Greet a friend, count, order churros con chocolate,
describe your family, and sing along the way. You will
listen, speak, read and write in every unit, and you are
never asked to copy a printed answer, because there are
none.

**A S T O R Y I N E V E R Y U N I T**

Every unit tells one story that runs to the end of the book.
You meet the same characters again and again, so the
words stay with you.

**S p a n i s h · Y e a r 1 · A 1 a n d A 2**

For teachers: nothing in this book is a test. The I can… boxes are the pupil’s own check,
and the QR codes give the audio for every listening activity.
