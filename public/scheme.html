Executive Summary: What Does Each Book Have?

1. Does each book have its own .md file?

YES, in two layers!

• Web & AI Layer: A single synced companion public/markdown/{slug}.md (125 files, auto-checked by git pre-commit hook md_sync.py) powers the reading assistant.
• Workshop Master: Inside MARKDOWN/ with per-unit files (00-front-matter.md, 01-unit-1.md...).

2. Does each book have an image / preview folder?

YES!

• Web Previews: Dedicated folder public/library/{slug}/preview/ holding 01.webp, 02.webp, and last.webp.
• Cover Art: cover.webp in library, plus master artwork in public/collection-covers/.
• Print Wraps: cover-wrap-amazon.png/.pdf for Amazon KDP.

3. Does each book have an input source file?

YES!

• School scheme of work / curriculum sheet: public/inputs/{slug} - input.xlsx (or .pdf).
• Stored in canonical workshop tree under PDF/Input/ as the authoritative scope & sequence reference.

4. Does each book have a BookVault print pack?

YES (derived on demand):

• Folder public/library/{slug}/bookvault/ containing Ghostscript PDF/X interior (-text-file.pdf), 300 DPI exact spine cover (-cover-file.pdf), metadata (build.json), and upload spec (.txt).

📝 2. AI & Text Companion: public/markdown/{slug}.md
Editable Source of Truth
public/markdown/{slug}.md (125 files total)
Every single Prime Book has an exact markdown companion in public/markdown/. It contains the full structured text, unit headers, and page breakdowns of the book. Synchronization contract: tools/md_sync.py runs as a pre-commit hook and compares word sets per page between the PDF and the MD. If drift occurs, the commit is blocked until updated. This allows Hermes (the AI assistant) to search sentences, cite page numbers, and assist teachers in real time without having to parse multi-megabyte binary PDFs on every turn.

2. Inside the Book: Architectural Page Anatomy

Standard A 2.0 Sequence

Every Prime Book follows a disciplined page-by-page structural grammar. Under Standard A 2.0, the contents page sits on physical page 3 and the teacher welcome sits on physical page 4:

PAGE 1
Front Cover

Full-bleed artwork, Prime School logo, Cambridge band badge, subject title, year numeral, "Student Manual" label, accent ochre rule, and full-height spine bar.

Pack A / B Voice
No text inside the artwork itself.
Logo is composite vector, not AI generated.
PAGE 2
Imprint & Copyright Page

Institutional copyright block (verbatim legal boilerplate), "Inside This Book" summary card, publisher contact (primeschool.pt), year, and print barcode.

Fixed Boilerplate
Never rewritten.
Preserves official ISBN and legal rights.
PAGE 3
Contents Page

Visual curriculum overview: unit colour cards, indented topic rows, right-aligned verified target page numbers. Unit count matches syllabus (not an arbitrary rigid bar).

Physical Page 3
Right-aligned verified targets.
Matches actual unit opener pages.
PAGE 4
Teacher Welcome & Cast / Hero Spread

Welcoming message to pupil, pedagogical invitation, and cast of characters (for Primary storybook) or conceptual hero figure (for Secondary).

Physical Page 4
Authentic teacher prose retained.
Prime School uniform on pupil characters.
PAGES 5–7
Front Matter & Preparation

"How to use this book" (symbol legend, icon vocabulary), subject safety guidelines, workspace setup, and year-at-a-glance roadmap.

Pedagogical Scaffolding
Establishes panel visual conventions once.
PAGES 8+
Curriculum Units (The Learning Core)

Each unit features:
• Unit Opener: Full page flooded in unit palette deep tone, large unit number, learning outcomes pills.
• Get Started: Curiosity hook, prior-knowledge question.
• Concept + Model: Instructional exposition + accurate diagram/illustration + caption.
• Your Turn: Active workspace, hands-on activity, drawing/writing area.
• Extension / Challenge: "Go a bit further" thinking task.
• Applied Project: Real-world synthesis and making.
• Reflection & Word Bank: "I can" statements and compact visual recall cards.

Page Furniture Standard
47 pt running-header band at y=0.
1.8 pt ochre rule (#9a6a12) under band.
24 pt circular folio badge at (550, 758).
Unit color palette throughout.
BACK MATTER
Consolidation, Glossary & Credits

Year portfolio / synthesis, Illustrated Glossary (2 columns × 3 cards with image, headword, and definition), Sources & Image Credits with verifiable URLs/licences, and closing reflection.

Audit Invariant
Traceable citations.
Legitimate provenance for all diagrams.
LAST PAGE
Back Cover (Even Page Parity Rule)

Solid panel in book theme palette, blurb summarizing the year, "Inside This Book" feature pill, Prime School publisher mark, and barcode area. Must end on an EVEN page number (e.g. 72, 88, 120) so the flipbook reader shows the back cover standalone.

Strict Invariant: Even Parity
US Letter (612 × 792 pt).
No odd final page counts.

3. Art Direction & Design Systems

Pack A vs Pack B

Pack A: "Beatrix Potter" Storybook

Scope: Years 1 to 6 (Lower & Upper Primary)

  • Medium: Gentle hand-drawn ink outlines with loose, soft watercolour washes on warm paper texture. No harsh 3D or flat vector clip art.
  • Subject Matter: Friendly animal characters wearing human clothing (school blazers, aprons, painter smocks) or school pupils wearing the official Prime School uniform.
  • Palette: Cream (#FBF6EC), Sage (#A8BF9B), Terracotta (#C97B5A), Butter (#F0CE7A), Dusky Blue (#8FA8C7), Ink (#3E3A35).
  • Typography: Fredoka (rounded display headings) + Andika (clean, highly legible pupil reading body) + Caveat (teacher annotations).
  • Prompt Voice: "Soft children's storybook illustration in a Beatrix Potter style: {subject}, {action}. Gentle hand-drawn ink outlines, loose watercolour washes, warm muted palette, rounded friendly shapes, paper texture, no text, no hard shadows, cozy and calm."

Pack B: "Geometrical" Academic

Scope: Years 7 to 13 (Secondary & Advanced Levels)

  • Medium: Crisp flat vector shapes with subtle grain, sophisticated layout, academic diagrams. No cartoon animals or hand-drawn sketches.
  • Subject Matter: Central bold geometric forms carrying subject-specific instruments: circuits for Computing, compass & protractor for Maths, DNA/microscope for Science, maps for Humanities.
  • Palette: Off-white (#F7F8FA), Deep Teal (#1F6F6B), Burnt Orange (#D2703C), Slate Blue (#4B5D7A), Charcoal (#1F2933).
  • Typography: Strong geometric display sans + neutral reading sans body.
  • Prompt Voice: "Minimal flat vector illustration for a secondary-school textbook: {subject}, {concept}. Bold geometric shapes, crisp edges, deep teal / burnt orange / slate / charcoal on off-white, subtle grain, no gradients, no text, no cartoon mascots, clean and academic."
