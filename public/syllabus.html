Where this comes from. Two sources, and every title says which one it is on. Where a title has an input spreadsheet — the school's own scheme of work, kept at public/inputs/<slug> - input.<ext> — its rows are expanded exactly as they stand: Unit, then its Subunits in order, then the term, revision and assessment sections that belong to no unit. Where the input is a PDF document, or there is no input at all, the list comes from the book itself instead — it is captured by opening the book in the reader and reading the panel, so it is the same list you see under Sections, with the reader's own page numbers. Each such block says so. Blank pages are the scheme's own blanks; nothing here is inferred, and no title mixes the two sources. A title with no list yet is one nothing can be read from — the 2-page shells, the PDFs with no contents page, and the books whose contents page the reader cannot turn into a unit list (contents page unreadable; the reader shows the same, and no bare number is dressed up as a unit here).
