The imprint page, standardised

One design (the index card), one colour per year taken from the year stripe on the cover: amber for Year 1, sky blue for Year 2, orange for Year 3, green for Year 4. Page 2 of every Year 1 to Year 4 book now carries it. Four examples per year are shown below; open any book and turn to page 2 to see its own.
