holds for Year 1, and (B) the published **Collins** and **Cambridge University Press** Year 1

Sources: Collins International Primary Schools Catalogue (official Collins PDF, prices are the
catalogue's, 2023); Cambridge University Press product/bookshop pages; Cambridge bookshop
(9781009354158); retailer listings for the remaining CUP items (flagged below).

### B6. Collins early years feed-in (age 3–5, i.e. the year before Year 1)

Collins International Foundation and Foundation Plus — English, maths and science activity
books, reading anthologies (one per term for English) and teacher's guides, by Peter Clarke,
Arabella Koopman and Fiona MacGregor. Activity books, anthologies and teacher's guides each;
full ISBN list only on the Collins online order form (`collins.co.uk/intcatalogues`).

### B7. Collins reading schemes relevant at Year 1

- **Collins Big Cat** — levelled readers. Year 1 bands in the scheme: Green (Band 5), Orange
  (Band 6), Turquoise (Band 7), with Red/Yellow/Blue as the lower Year 1 range; ~16–32pp,
  word counts from ~20 up. Over 1,000 levelled readers in the programme.
- **Peapod Readers** — 200 illustrated ESL readers, CEFR Pre-A1 to A1, aligned to Cambridge
  English Qualifications Pre-A1 Starters and A1 Movers.
- **Little Wandle** phonics readers and classroom resources (DfE-validated synthetic phonics).

## Caveats

- Collins ISBNs come from Collins' own catalogue PDF; that edition is 2023, so reprints may
  have moved prices and a few components (Foundation/Foundation Plus, Big Cat individual
  titles) publish their full ISBN lists only on the Collins order form.
- CUP ISBNs for the four items marked "retail listing only" were read off retailer pages and
  not confirmed on cambridge.org — treat those four as needing a check before ordering.
- Schemas differ: Cambridge "Stage 1" = school Year 1 = age 5–6; Collins uses "Level 1" for the
  international primary courses and book bands (Lilac–) for the readers.
