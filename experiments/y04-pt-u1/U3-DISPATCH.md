- **Pages:** 82–87, colour plum.

- **Header pill:** `<i>2</i>Varal 2 · Um poema sobre um sentimento`.
- **Images:** `u3-04-feeling.jpg` (square: Amara at a rainy window), `u3-05-feelings.jpg` (wide strip of four emotions).
- **Scheme lines:**
  - Leitura: a complete poem about a feeling.
  - EL: understand the feeling and the words that show it; read the poem to the class.
  - Escrita: recreate the poem, or write four lines about a feeling, with a title.
  - Gramática: the adjective in the noun phrase; agreement in gender and number.
- **Rights:** both poets are IN COPYRIGHT, so print no line of either. The teacher chooses one poem about a feeling, either from *O Livro da Tila* (Matilde Rosa Araújo) or from *Poemas da Mentira e da Verdade* (Luísa Ducla Soares). The pupils copy the poem by hand onto a decorated «Cartão do poema» page, and every later task works on their copy.
- **Author facts:** VERIFY each one with a web search before printing it.
- **Suggested pages:**
  - p. 82: poet cards and a «Hoje, como me sinto?» warm-up with the feelings strip.
  - p. 83: «Cartão do poema», the copy page. It has a title line, the author, around 16 generous lines, a decorated border, and room for a small illustration.
  - p. 84: EL. A feelings thermometer or wheel, «as palavras que mostram o sentimento» (hunting words in their copy), and a reading-to-the-class card.
  - p. 85: oral reading. Pause and intonation marks, practice steps, a peer rubric.
  - p. 86: grammar. Adjectives in the noun phrase (for example «um dia cinzento», «a chuva fria») and agreement in gender and number (cinzento/cinzenta/cinzentos/cinzentas; feliz/felizes; triste/tristes). Invariable-gender adjectives must be classified exactly.
  - p. 87: writing. Recreate the poem, or write four lines about a feeling with a title, using a feelings-to-images bank (a feeling → a colour, a weather, an animal).
- **Deliverables:** the pages, `text/u3-v2.md`, and `notes/u3-v2.md`.

## Varal 3 · Um poema sobre o mar (scheme 3.2)
- **Pages:** 88–93, colour blue.
- **Build command:** `--out u3v3`.
- **Header pill:** `<i>3</i>Varal 3 · Um poema sobre o mar`.
- **Images:** `u3-06-sea.jpg` (wide: the whole cast reciting at the beach), `u3-07-shells.jpg` (square).
- **Scheme lines:**
  - Leitura: a short poem to read in chorus.
  - EL: find the repetition and the rhyme; say whether you liked it and why.
  - Escrita: a three-sentence summary of the poem, or a new four-line poem.
  - Gramática: listing with commas; spelling of the words in the poem.
- **Text:** our own poem, about 16–20 short lines. It needs a refrain, rhyme, an enumeration with commas, and sea vocabulary. Lay it out as a choral-reading score with the voices Todos / Grupo A / Grupo B / Uma voz.
- **Audio:** `audio/o-mar-tem.txt` and `.mp3` (pt-PT-RaquelNeural), with a QR code `qr-o-mar-tem.svg`. Test the QR decode.
- **Frame:** a class trip to the beach (Cascais, or the beach at the mouth of the Tejo). Do not name a real school.
- **Suggested pages:**
  - pp. 88–89: the poem and the choral score.
  - p. 90: EL. Repetition, rhyme, and «gostei porque…».
  - p. 91: grammar. Listing with commas, including the rule for «e» before the last item.
  - p. 92: spelling of the poem's words. Look–say–cover–write–check, s/ss/ç, the nasal sounds and «nh».
  - p. 93: writing. Option A is a three-sentence summary; option B is a new four-line sea poem.
- **Deliverables:** the pages, `text/u3-v3.md`, and `notes/u3-v3.md` (≤ 300 words).
