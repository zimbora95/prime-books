# 02 — Teaching plan

## Big question
**Para que servem os textos que encontramos todos os dias?**
(What are the texts we meet every day for?)

## Narrative spine: "Mensagens na Rua do Limoeiro"
In Rua do Limoeiro (Alfama, Lisbon), a windy morning blows an envelope out of the old red post box. The envelope has no stamp and says only "Rosa". Inês, Tomás, Amara and the dog Farrusco take it to Dona Rosa's kiosk rather than opening it (an ethics beat). Inside is a **letter** from Senhor Joaquim, the retired postman, and an **invitation**: the old post box will become a street library. The local radio broadcasts the **news**, and the neighbourhood paper prints it. Farrusco steals the first book, and Amara turns that into a **comic strip**. The children then write their own letters with an opinion. They finish by producing **their own class newspaper**.

Each text type is met as a real object inside the story, used for a real purpose, before it is taken apart. This follows the order *purpose → structure → practice → own production*.

## Outcomes (checkable)
By the end of Unit 1 the child can:
1. Listen to a short news item and record who, what, where, when and what for.
2. Say what a letter, an invitation, a news item and a comic are for, and name their main parts.
3. Predict what a text is about from its title and illustration, then check the prediction.
4. Write a letter (place and date, greeting, body, closing, signature) that gives an opinion and at least one reason.
5. Write an invitation that answers who, what, when and where.
6. Write and punctuate declarative, interrogative and exclamative sentences, and turn affirmative sentences into negative ones.

## Sequence (gradual release)
| Stop | Pages | I do (model) | We do | You do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arrival | 1–4 | map of the unit | diagnostic sort | "o que já sei" |
| Story | 5–6 | read aloud | predict and discuss | check the prediction |
| 1 Carta | 7–8 | annotated letter | label the parts | address an envelope |
| 2 Convite | 9–10 | annotated invitation | fix Tomás's invitation | design an invitation |
| 3 Notícia | 11–12 | radio news (QR) | listening grid | 5 questions + fact/opinion |
| 4 BD | 13–14 | comic strip | name the parts | draw panel 5 |
| Gramática | 15–18 | discovery with voices | board game | proofread Tomás |
| Escrita | 19–20 | Amara's model letter | plan together | own letter + checklist |
| Projeto | 21 | roles | group newsroom | publish and present |
| Fecho | 22 | — | — | "Já consigo…" |

Difficulty rises inside each stop (★ → ★★ → ★★★) and across the unit: recognise → transform → produce.
Spaced retrieval: the parts of the letter come back on pp. 10, 19 and 20. The sentence types are found in the comic (p. 14) before they are taught (p. 15), then practised in the game (p. 17) and the proofreading (p. 18).

## Differentiation
- ★ support: matching, circling, sentence starters.
- ★★ core: short written answers and transformations.
- ★★★ challenge: fact vs opinion, *ainda → já não*, combined type + polarity, open writing.

## Assessment
- Diagnostic: p. 4 sort.
- Formative: a check at each stop, the checklist on p. 20 (self and peer), the game on p. 17 (observation).
- Summative / product: the class newspaper (p. 21) with a 6-point success list.
- Self-assessment: p. 22.

## Voice of the book
Warm, direct, second person singular (*tu*). Instructions are one imperative verb, always the same: **Lê. Ouve. Conversa. Escreve. Sublinha. Liga. Desenha. Joga.**
