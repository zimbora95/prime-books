# Unidade 5 · A Caça ao Tesouro da Rua (Revisões do ano): plan

**Scheme of work.** "Unidade 5 - Revisões Anuais: Revisões do 3.º ano: notícia, carta, convite, banda desenhada, conto, diário, poema e cena".
**Rules.** Follow `U2-BRIEF.md` exactly. **Language.** PT-PT, AO90. **Readers.** Children aged 8–9.

## Big idea
It is June 2027, the Santo António festival, and the end of the school year. Sr. Joaquim leaves the friends a hand-drawn treasure map with **8 clues (pistas)**. Each clue hides a new short text of one of the eight text types studied this year. To unlock the next clue, you read, answer, fix and write.

The treasure, found under the lemon tree, is a box with a letter from Sr. Joaquim. It holds the key to a new estante in the Pequena Biblioteca, where the pupils' own texts from the whole year will live. This links back to Units 1 and 2.

- **Big question:** *O que aprendi a ler e a escrever este ano?*
- **Unit colour:** red.

## Two-page pattern for each pista
**Page A: the text and the reading.**
- A NEW short original text of the type (120–220 words), set in the street at festival time.
- A box «Lembra-te» recalling the text type's features (with a page reference to where it was taught: Unit 1 pp. 7–14; Unit 2 pp. 27–68; Unit 3 pp. 75–93; Unit 4 pp. 99–114).
- 2–3 comprehension tasks.

**Page B: grammar, then writing.**
- 2 grammar/spelling review tasks, spiralling the year's content (see the mapping below).
- One short writing task in that text type.
- A clue-solved stamp: «Pista N resolvida!» plus one letter of the secret word **LIMOEIRO** (8 letters). Pista 1 = L, 2 = I, 3 = M, 4 = O, 5 = E, 6 = I, 7 = R, 8 = O. On p. 137 the pupils write the secret word, which is where the treasure is.

## Content for each pista (texts are all original unless marked)
1. **Notícia**
   - **Text:** the street theatre show from Unit 4 was a success («Teatro na Rua do Limoeiro enche a rua de aplausos»). Newspaper *O Limoeiro*; use a weekday verified with Python (June 2027).
   - **Tasks:** quem / o quê / quando / onde / como / porquê; título and lead.
   - **Grammar:** types of sentence (declarativa / interrogativa / exclamativa); affirmative → negative.
   - **Writing:** a 3-sentence news item about the arraial.
2. **Carta**
   - **Text:** a letter to Amara from her avó in Cabo Verde (local, data, saudação, corpo, despedida, assinatura; the P.S. announces a visit).
   - **Grammar:** possessive and demonstrative determiners; the pretérito perfeito of ser/ir/ter/fazer.
   - **Writing:** Amara's reply (a short letter with all parts).
3. **Convite**
   - **Text:** an invitation to the Arraial da Rua do Limoeiro (who invites, what, when, where, what to bring, RSVP).
   - **Task:** find the MISSING piece of information in a second, incomplete invitation.
   - **Grammar:** the future (farei, iremos…); the adverbs of time and place.
   - **Writing:** an invitation to your own class end-of-year party.
4. **Banda desenhada**
   - **Image:** u5-03-comic (three panels in one wide image; crop into three frames with object-position 0% / 50% / 100%).
   - Speech balloons, thought bubble, onomatopoeia, caption.
   - **Tasks:** order, fill the balloons.
   - **Grammar:** punctuation marks (! ? . , :); interjections/onomatopoeias.
   - **Writing:** a 4th panel (draw it and write the balloon).
5. **Conto**
   - **Text:** «O leão e o rato» (Aesop's fable, retold in our own words, public domain; image u5-04-lion-mouse). Beginning / middle / end; personagens / espaço / tempo / ação; moral.
   - **Grammar:** the tenses (presente / pretérito perfeito / futuro); the connectors antes / depois.
   - **Writing:** change the ending, or tell it from the mouse's point of view.
6. **Diário**
   - **Text:** Inês's diary, 2 entries from the Santo António week (image u5-05-ines-diary). Fez vs sentiu.
   - **Grammar:** connectors (e, mas, porque, depois, então); word families; prefixes and suffixes.
   - **Writing:** one diary entry about your last school day.
7. **Poema**
   - **Text:** 3 traditional Santo António quadras (tradição popular; cross-check each with 2 sources), plus one new original quadra.
   - **Tasks:** rhyme, stressed syllable, verso/estrofe.
   - **Grammar:** adjectives and agreement; comma in lists.
   - **Writing:** a quadra for a manjerico, from 4 words.
8. **Cena**
   - **Text:** a short scene (about 12 lines, 3 characters) in which the friends dig under the lemon tree and find the treasure box; stage directions.
   - **Grammar:** subject and predicate in the lines; punctuation of the dramatic text; the verbs dizer / perguntar / responder (turning 2 lines into narrative with travessão).
   - **Writing:** continue the scene: what is in the box? Use 3 lines and 1 stage direction.
