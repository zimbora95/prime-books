# Unidade 4 · A Rua Sobe ao Palco: teaching plan and page plan

**Scheme of work.** "Unidade 4 - Texto Dramático", with two sub-units: 4.1 (Cena: a Lua) and 4.2 (Cena na escola).
**Readers.** Children aged 8–9, native speakers. **Language.** PT-PT, AO90.
**Rules.** Follow `U2-BRIEF.md` exactly; it applies to Unit 4 too.

## Big idea
It is April 2027, and the Rua do Limoeiro is putting on a show. The friends build a small street theatre from crates and an old red blanket. They rehearse two scenes in Dona Rosa's back yard. On the last Saturday of April they perform for the whole street.
- **Big question:** *Como é que uma história ganha vida num palco?*
- **Unit colour:** orng (opener, map, project page).

## Scheme lines, to be covered exactly
**4.1 Cena: a Lua**
- **Leitura:** the features of the dramatic text: who speaks, and the stage directions (entra, sai, em voz baixa).
- **EL:** read the whole scene, then read it in parts.
- **Escrita:** continue the scene with new lines and one stage direction.
- **Gramática:** interrogative and exclamative sentences; the verbs dizer, perguntar, responder.

**4.2 Cena na escola**
- **Leitura:** a whole scene with up to four characters.
- **EL:** act out the scene, and say what changed from the beginning to the end.
- **Escrita:** a dramatic text with a title, lines and stage directions, written from a plan.
- **Gramática:** subject and predicate in the lines; punctuation of the dramatic text.

## Texts (both our own, so no rights issues)
**Conventions for our dramatic texts**, used consistently:
- Title, then a character list («Personagens:»).
- The opening stage direction in italics inside parentheses (setting and time).
- Each line starts with the NAME in small caps or bold, followed by a colon.
- Stage directions inside a line go in parentheses, in italics: «(em voz baixa)», «(entra a correr)», «(sai)».
- No travessão in the dramatic text. Say this explicitly in the punctuation lesson.

**Cena 1 · «A Lua no chafariz»** (≈ 450–550 words, 5 characters)
- **Characters:** Inês, Tomás, Amara, Farrusco (who only barks «Ão! Ão!» and has stage directions), and A LUA (a voice from above, spoken by a pupil standing on a chair).
- **Plot:**
  - At night, Farrusco sees the Moon in the basin of the fountain and thinks it has fallen.
  - The friends try to "rescue" it: a net, a bucket, and Tomás's plan.
  - Each attempt breaks the reflection into ripples, and they panic.
  - Amara looks up. The Moon speaks («Estou aqui em cima, meninos!») and explains the reflection kindly.
  - Ending: they wish the Moon good night and leave, and Farrusco still barks at the water.
- **Uses:**
  - lots of questions and exclamations, for the grammar;
  - the stage directions entra, sai, em voz baixa, a rir, assustado;
  - a clear place for continuing the scene. After the Moon leaves, a cloud covers it. What do they say next?

**Cena 2 · «Na escola»** (≈ 450–550 words, **4 characters**)
- **Characters:** Professora Glória (a hedgehog, new; kind), Tomás, Inês, Amara.
- **Plot:**
  - It is Monday morning. Tomás must present the "notícia da semana" to the class, but he is nervous and has lost his page.
  - Inês and Amara help him: breathe, remember, tell it as a story.
  - He presents the news (the street theatre!) with confidence and a joke.
  - Change from start to end: nervous and lost → confident and proud. The EL task is built on this.
- **Stage directions:** entra, sai, em voz baixa, a tremer, a sorrir, todos batem palmas.

## Page pattern for each scene (8 pages)
1. p1: Antes de ler (anticipate from the title and picture) + «O que é um texto dramático?» with an annotated example (labels: título, personagens, fala, indicação cénica).
2. pp2–3: the scene (2 pages, with the illustration).
3. p4: Compreender: EL and Leitura as the scheme states them.
4. p5: Ler em papéis / Representar: role cards, voice and gesture, a rubric. In Cena 2, «o que mudou» (a before/after organiser).
5. p6: Gramática 1.
6. p7: Gramática 2 or a word workshop.
7. p8: Escrita: a plan, the lines with names and directions, a checklist.
