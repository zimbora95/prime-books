## Learners: what 8–9-year-old native speakers bring
- They read fluently but unevenly. Short paragraphs, generous leading and 14–18 pt body text keep them going.
- They know letters and invitations from home life (birthday invitations, notes, WhatsApp) but rarely name the parts.
- They focus for about 10–15 minutes per activity. Pages must alternate between reading, talking, writing and moving.
- They are motivated by mysteries, animals (especially dogs), humour, collecting, games, and doing "real" grown-up jobs (reporter, postman).

## Typical mistakes to target
- Omitting the final full stop, or using "!" everywhere.
- Question intonation without "?" in writing, e.g. *Onde está o cão.*
- A capital letter after a comma; no capital at the start of a sentence.
- Negation: the misplaced *não* (*O Farrusco gosta não*), and *ainda → já não*.
- Letter conventions: forgetting the date/place and the closing, and mixing up *remetente/destinatário*.
- Invitations that leave out *quando* or *onde* (by far the most common real-life error).
- Dialogue punctuation: the travessão (—) is often missing.
