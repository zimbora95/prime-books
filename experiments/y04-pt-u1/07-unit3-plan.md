# Unidade 3 · A Rua que Rima: teaching plan and page plan

**Scheme of work (source).** "Unidade 3 - Texto Poético - Lengalenga e quadra tradicional", with sub-units 3.1 and 3.2.
**Pupils.** Aged 8–9, native speakers. **Language.** PT-PT, AO90.
**Rules.** Follow `U2-BRIEF.md` exactly: design system, ground rules, build and QA, deliverables. It applies to Unit 3 too.

## Big idea
It is February 2027 and the Rua do Limoeiro is grey and rainy. Dona Rosa finds, at the back of her kiosk, a box of paper flags carrying the traditional quadras from last June's Santo António festival. The friends decide to hang a **Varal dos Poemas** across the street (a string of poems pegged like washing between the lamp posts) to cheer up the winter.

The unit has three «varais» (strings):
1. lengalengas and traditional quadras;
2. a poem about a feeling;
3. a poem about the sea, read in chorus on a trip to the beach.

- **Big question:** *Como é que as palavras podem cantar?* The answer runs through the unit as rhythm, rhyme, repetition and the voice.
- **Unit colour** (opener and map): teal.

## Scheme lines, to be covered exactly
**Varal 1 (Unidade 3)**
- **Leitura:** the features of the poetic text: lengalenga, traditional quadra and short poem.
- **EL:** anticipate the theme, mark the rhyme, and say the poem in public.
- **Escrita:** a quadra with rhyme, from a plan of four words.
- **Gramática:** rhyme and the stressed syllable; capital letters and punctuation in the line of verse.

**Varal 2 (3.1)**
- **Leitura:** a complete poem about a feeling, by Luísa Ducla Soares or Matilde Rosa Araújo.
- **EL:** understand the feeling and the words that show it; read the poem to the class.
- **Escrita:** recreate the poem, or write four lines about a feeling, with a title.
- **Gramática:** the adjective in the noun phrase; agreement in gender and number.

**Varal 3 (3.2)**
- **Leitura:** a short poem to read in chorus.
- **EL:** find the repetition and the rhyme; say whether you liked it and why.
- **Escrita:** a three-sentence summary of the poem, or a new four-line poem.
- **Gramática:** listing with commas; spelling of the words in the poem.

## Rights and texts
- **Lengalengas and quadras populares** are anonymous oral tradition and in the public domain. Use well-attested versions: take them from ditos.pt or lapismagico.com and **cross-check each against a second source**. Candidates:
  - «Era uma vez um gato maltês…»;
  - «Hoje é domingo, pede cachimbo…»;
  - counting-out rhymes («Pim, pam, pum…»);
  - traditional quadras, including the Santo António ones and «Fui ao mar buscar laranjas…».
  Give each one a credit line: «Tradição popular portuguesa».
- **Luísa Ducla Soares and Matilde Rosa Araújo are IN COPYRIGHT.** Print NO poem and NO line by either of them. Instead:
  - the teacher chooses **one poem about a feeling** from *As Fadas Verdes* (Matilde Rosa Araújo; on the official Educação Literária list for 3.º ano, "escolher 8 poemas") or from *Poemas da Mentira e da Verdade* (Luísa Ducla Soares);
  - the pupils **copy it by hand** onto a decorated page in the book (a «Cartão do poema» page, doubling as a handwriting task);
  - all further tasks work on their own copy.
- **Author facts: verify each one with a web search before printing it; print only what you have confirmed.**
  - Matilde Rosa Araújo: Lisbon, 1921–2010; teacher and writer; *As Fadas Verdes* (verify the date before printing it).
  - Luísa Ducla Soares: born in Lisbon in 1939; poems and stories for children; *Poemas da Mentira e da Verdade*.
- **The sea poem (Varal 3) is our own**, titled «O mar tem…» or better, about 16–20 short lines. Requirements:
  - strong **repetition** (a refrain) and **rhyme**;
  - an **enumeration with commas** (for the grammar);
  - vocabulary for spelling (onda, areia, gaivota, concha, espuma, farol, maresia, peixinho, barco);
  - set out for **choral reading**: Todos / Grupo A / Grupo B / Uma voz;
  - QR audio, read by one TTS voice (pt-PT-RaquelNeural).
