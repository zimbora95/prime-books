# Unidade 6 · Mostra o que Sabes (Avaliação): plan

**Scheme of work.** "Unidade 6 - Avaliação: Testes / trabalhos / correção: leitura em voz alta, narrativa curta revista, opinião com uma razão, presente, pretérito perfeito e futuro".
**Rules.** Follow `U2-BRIEF.md` exactly. **Language.** PT-PT, AO90. **Pupils.** Aged 8–9.

## Big idea
At the end of June 2027, the friends show Sr. Joaquim everything they wrote during the year. Now it is the pupil's turn to *show what they know*.
- **Tone:** calm, encouraging, never scary. Each «prova» opens with «O que vou mostrar» (child-friendly success criteria) and ends with a self-check.
- **Teacher materials:** scoring rubrics with points go in the teacher notes (p. 152). The pupil pages carry child-friendly versions.

## Prova 1 · Ler em voz alta (pp. 143–144)
**p. 143**
- A NEW original text of about 170–190 words, to be read aloud to the teacher: «O farol que piscava ao contrário» (a short funny story at the beach). It has dialogue, a question, an exclamation, and a list with commas.
- The text is set at 15 pt with line numbers every 5 lines, and a word count printed at the end of each line in the margin (small, grey), so the teacher can record words read correctly per minute.
- Image u6-02-reading as a small vignette.

**p. 144**
- «Como li?»: a pupil self-assessment. Faces for volume, ritmo, respeitar a pontuação, expressão, and palavras bem lidas.
- The teacher's record box: date, time taken, words read correctly, notes.
- 4 comprehension questions on the text:
  - 1 literal;
  - 1 about vocabulary in context;
  - 1 inference;
  - 1 opinion.

## Prova 2 · Uma narrativa revista (pp. 145–147)
- **p. 145:** the picture prompt (u6-01-kite, wide), the task («Conta como os amigos recuperaram o papagaio de papel»), and a plan with Início / Meio / Fim (quem, onde, quando, problema, tentativas, solução).
- **p. 146:** the first draft (about 14 lines at 9 mm), plus a sidebar with the revision code:
  - O = ortografia
  - P = pontuação
  - M = maiúscula
  - ^ = falta uma palavra
  - ~ = repetição
  These symbols are safe to use; draw them as styled letters in circles.
- **p. 147:**
  - a revision checklist, done in pairs;
  - the final clean version (about 12 lines);
  - «O que melhorei».

## Prova 3 · A minha opinião (p. 148)
- A short review card by Amara about a book from the Pequena Biblioteca. It is a model of about 60 words, with «Na minha opinião… porque…».
- The task: choose one text from this year (a list of titles with page numbers) and write an opinion with ONE clear reason.
- A frame with: sentence starters; star rating (SVG stars); «A minha razão é…».
- A child rubric: the opinion is said / there is one reason / the reason is linked with «porque» / punctuation.

## Prova 4 · Os tempos dos verbos (pp. 149–150)
The forms must be EXACT, with the persons eu / tu / ele, ela / nós / eles, elas.

**p. 149**
- Recognition: sort 12 verb forms into ontem / hoje / amanhã.
- Complete a table for ser, ir, ter, fazer, estar in the presente, pretérito perfeito and futuro, for the persons eu and nós.

**p. 150**
- A short text from Tomás's diary written in the present, to be rewritten in the pretérito perfeito (6 verbs underlined).
- Complete sentences with the future: «Amanhã, nós ___ (ir) à praia.»
- Time markers: ontem / hoje / amanhã / no próximo ano / na semana passada.

## Aprendo com os meus erros (p. 151)
- A correction table with three columns: o meu erro | a forma certa | porquê (a regra).
- «As minhas três palavras difíceis» (look, say, cover, write, check).
- Goals for next year, written neutrally: «No próximo ano, quero…». (Year 4 corresponds to the Portuguese 3.º ano, so next year is the 4.º ano.)
- A final bookshelf of everything done this year.

## Teacher notes (p. 152)
Rubrics with points for each prova:
- **Leitura em voz alta:** words correct per minute, with the benchmark stated neutrally («registe e compare com o início do ano»). Do not invent official benchmarks.
- **Narrativa:** 20 points (structure, content, language, spelling and punctuation, revision).
- **Opinião:** 10 points.
- **Verbos:** answers with points.

Also include accommodations: extra time, reading the questions aloud, and a scribe for pupils with motor difficulties.
